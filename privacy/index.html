Privacy policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Streaming Price Check does not ask you to create an account. It uses cookies and similar technologies for analytics, advertising and affiliate tracking, as described below.

What data is collected?

Standard analytics data such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, collected through Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics. If you use the contact form, we receive the details you submit.

Cookies and Google ads

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

Affiliate links

When you click an affiliate link, the destination site or affiliate network (such as Amazon, Impact or CJ) may set a cookie to credit the referral. Their privacy policies apply on their sites.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works without them.

Questions about privacy: use the contact page.
