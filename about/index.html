About Streaming Price Check

Streaming Price Check is an independent guide to US streaming services and devices, run by Sam Barth as part of the samcbarth.com network of sites.

What does this site do?

It tracks the plans and prices of the major streaming services, compares them head to head, explains which service fits which kind of viewer, and covers streaming news that affects what you pay.

How is it funded?

The site may earn commissions from affiliate links to streaming services and devices. Those commissions never change which services we list, the prices we show, or the order of our picks. See the affiliate disclosure.

How is content researched?

Reviews are research-based: official pricing pages, provider help centers and published reporting, all linked. Read how we research.
