Affiliate disclosure

Some links on Streaming Price Check may be affiliate links. If you sign up for a service or buy a device through one, this site may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Which links are affiliate links?

Buttons that send you to a streaming service (for example "Visit Netflix") and "Check price on Amazon" buttons may carry affiliate tracking. Affiliate links are marked with rel="sponsored". A disclosure line appears above the first such button on every page that has them.

Amazon Associates

As an Amazon Associate, this site earns from qualifying purchases once enrolled in the program. We do not display Amazon prices or ratings because they change constantly; the button takes you to Amazon to see the current price.

Does this affect recommendations?

No. Prices come from official provider pages, and picks are based on published lineups and prices. A service without an affiliate program is listed exactly the same way.
