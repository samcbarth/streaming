YouTube TV review: plans, prices and who it is for

By Sam Barth · Updated November 9, 2026 · Research-based review

YouTube TV's base plan is $82.99 a month for 100+ live channels, including local ABC, CBS, FOX and NBC in most markets, with unlimited DVR. Cheaper genre plans start at $64.99.

The base plan carries broadcast networks, ESPN and most major cable channels. DVR storage has no limit.

YouTube TV now sells smaller genre plans, such as Sports at $64.99 and Sports + News at $71.99.

It is the only way to get NFL Sunday Ticket as an add-on to a live TV service.
