Peacock review: plans, prices and who it is for

By Sam Barth · Updated November 9, 2026 · Research-based review

Peacock Premium costs $12.99 a month with ads and includes NBC live sports; Premium Plus is $19.99 ad-free. The $8.99 Select plan skips sports, movies and originals.

What should you know before subscribing?

Peacock raised prices on all three plans in August 2026, its fourth increase in four years.

Select is a stripped-down plan for current and past NBC and Bravo shows. Most people should start at Premium, which adds live sports, movies and Peacock originals.

Premium Plus removes most ads and adds downloads and a live feed of your local NBC station.
