Philo review: plans, prices and who it is for

By Sam Barth · Updated November 9, 2026 · Research-based review

Philo is the cheapest live TV service for entertainment channels: $25 a month for 70+ channels. It has no sports networks and no local channels.

What should you know before subscribing?

Philo focuses on entertainment and lifestyle cable networks and leaves out sports and news channels to keep its price low.

Bundle+ is $33 a month and adds extra services on top of the Essential lineup.

Pair Philo with an antenna and a sports service if you need those.
