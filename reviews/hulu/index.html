Hulu review: plans, prices and who it is for

By Sam Barth · Updated November 9, 2026 · Research-based review

Hulu is best bought as part of the Disney+, Hulu bundle: $12.99 a month with ads, only 50 cents more than Hulu alone at $12.49. Ad-free Hulu by itself costs $21.49.

Who is Hulu best for?

  • Next-day TV episodes and FX series
  • People who also want Disney+ (the bundle costs 50 cents more than Hulu alone)

Who should skip it?

  • Anyone who only wants one standalone ad-free service at a low price

What should you know before subscribing?

Hulu's own plans are $12.49 with ads and $21.49 without. Disney prices its bundles so that adding Disney+ costs very little, so most people should compare the bundle, not Hulu alone.

Hulu + Live TV is Disney's live TV package. It bundles live channels with Hulu, Disney+ and ESPN Select for $99.99 a month, and a live-only option lists at $98.99.

Hulu is strong on current TV: many network and FX series arrive the day after they air.
