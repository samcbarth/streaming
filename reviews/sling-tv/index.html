Sling TV review: plans, prices and who it is for

By Sam Barth · Updated November 9, 2026 · Research-based review

Sling TV is the lowest-cost live TV service with big cable networks: Orange or Blue is $45.99 a month, both together $60.99, and smaller Select and Essentials plans are $19.99.

What should you know before subscribing?

Sling Orange includes ESPN channels. Sling Blue has a larger lineup and some local channels in select markets.

The $19.99 Select and Essentials plans are pared-down lineups for people who want only a few channels.

Sling carries few local stations, so it works best with an over-the-air antenna.
