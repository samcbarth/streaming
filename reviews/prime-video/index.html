Prime Video review: plans, prices and who it is for

By Sam Barth · Updated November 9, 2026 · Research-based review

If you already have Amazon Prime ($14.99 a month or $139 a year), Prime Video is included with ads. Removing ads costs $4.99 a month more. Video-only Prime Video is $8.99.

What should you know before subscribing?

Prime Video comes with an Amazon Prime membership. Ads are on by default; the Ultra add-on removes them for $4.99 a month, up from $2.99 before 2026.

Live sports include Thursday Night Football and a package of NBA games.

The ad-free price listed in our tables ($19.98) is Prime plus Ultra.
