Fubo review: plans, prices and who it is for

By Sam Barth · Updated November 9, 2026 · Research-based review

Fubo is the sports-focused live TV service. Pro costs $88.99 a month and Elite $98.99, with first-month discounts. A Spanish-language Latino plan is $19.99.

What should you know before subscribing?

Fubo builds its plans around sports, including regional sports networks in some markets, which many rivals do not carry.

Pro is the main plan. Elite adds more channels.

Disney combined Hulu + Live TV with Fubo in 2025, but the two are still sold as separate services.
