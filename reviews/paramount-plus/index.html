Paramount+ review: plans, prices and who it is for

By Sam Barth · Updated November 9, 2026 · Research-based review

Paramount+ is the cheapest major service with a mostly ad-free plan: Premium is $13.99 a month and includes live local CBS. Essential with ads is $8.99.
