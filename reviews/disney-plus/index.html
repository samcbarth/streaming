Disney+ review: plans, prices and who it is for

By Sam Barth · Updated November 9, 2026 · Research-based review

Disney+ is the top family pick, but it is rarely worth buying alone: the Disney+, Hulu bundle with ads costs $12.99 a month, just 50 cents more than Disney+ by itself at $12.49.

What should you know before subscribing?

Disney+ raised prices in September 2026. The ad plan is $12.49 a month and Premium, which removes ads, is $21.49 or $214.99 a year.

The catalog centers on Disney animation, Pixar, Marvel, Star Wars and National Geographic. That makes it the most useful service for households with children.

Because Disney prices its bundles aggressively, compare the bundle prices before choosing a standalone plan.
