HBO Max review: plans, prices and who it is for

By Sam Barth · Updated November 9, 2026 · Research-based review

HBO Max starts at $10.99 a month with ads and $18.49 ad-free. It is the best pick for HBO originals and Warner Bros. movies; 4K needs Premium at $22.99.

Who is HBO Max best for?

  • HBO series and Warner Bros. films
  • Viewers who want prestige drama at a mid-range price

Who should skip it?

  • Families who need a large kids catalog
  • 4K on a budget

What should you know before subscribing?

HBO Max sells three plans. Basic with Ads and Standard both stream in Full HD on two devices. Premium adds 4K and four streams.

Annual billing saves money on every plan: $109.99, $184.99 and $229.99 a year.

If you already pay for Disney+ and Hulu, the three-service bundle is usually cheaper than adding HBO Max separately.
