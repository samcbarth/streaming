Netflix review: plans, prices and who it is for

By Sam Barth · Updated November 9, 2026 · Research-based review

Netflix is the safest single pick if you want a large original library and no live TV. The ad plan costs $8.99 a month; ad-free starts at $19.99, and 4K needs the $26.99 Premium plan.

What should you know before subscribing?

Netflix has three US plans. The ad-supported plan is the cheapest way in, and it streams in up to 1080p on two screens at once. Standard removes ads at the same quality. Premium is the only plan with 4K, HDR and four simultaneous streams.

Netflix does not offer live channels, so it pairs well with a live TV service or an antenna. Its live programming is limited to specific events and WWE Raw.

If you share an account with someone outside your household, Netflix charges for an extra member slot instead of allowing free password sharing.
