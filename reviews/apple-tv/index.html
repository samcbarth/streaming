Apple TV review: plans, prices and who it is for

By Sam Barth · Updated November 9, 2026 · Research-based review

Apple TV (the service formerly called Apple TV+) costs $14.99 a month or $119 a year, with no ads and 4K included. It is a small, originals-only catalog.

Who is Apple TV best for?

  • High-quality originals without ads
  • MLS and Formula 1 fans

Who should skip it?

  • People who want a big back catalog of licensed movies and shows

What should you know before subscribing?

Apple raised the price from $12.99 to $14.99 a month on August 28, 2026. The annual plan rose from $99 to $119.

Every subscription is ad-free and includes 4K HDR. The library is almost entirely Apple originals.

Apple One bundles Apple TV with other Apple services and can be cheaper if you pay for iCloud or Apple Music anyway.
