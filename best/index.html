Best streaming services by need

Short, sourced answers to the most common streaming questions, ranked from published lineups and prices checked November 9, 2026.

Best streaming service for sports

For the most sports in one place, YouTube TV ($82.99) or Fubo (Pro $88.99) are the strongest picks because they carry local networks plus ESPN.

Best streaming service for kids

Disney+ is the best fit for most families because of its Disney, Pixar and Marvel library.

Cheapest streaming services

The cheapest major paid plans are Netflix Standard with ads, Peacock Select, Paramount+ Essential and Prime Video standalone, all at $8.99 a month.

Best live TV streaming service

YouTube TV is the best all-around cable replacement at $82.99 a month: local networks in most markets, ESPN, 100+ channels and unlimited DVR.
