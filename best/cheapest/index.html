Cheapest streaming services (November 2026)

By Sam Barth · Prices checked November 9, 2026

What is the cheapest streaming service?

The cheapest major paid plans are Netflix Standard with ads, Peacock Select, Paramount+ Essential and Prime Video standalone, all at $8.99 a month. The cheapest ad-free plan among the big services is Paramount+ Premium at $13.99.
