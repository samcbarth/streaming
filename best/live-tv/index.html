By Sam Barth · Prices checked November 9, 2026

What is the best live TV streaming service to replace cable?

YouTube TV is the best all-around cable replacement at $82.99 a month: local networks in most markets, ESPN, 100+ channels and unlimited DVR. Sling TV ($45.99) and Philo ($25) are cheaper but leave out local channels.
