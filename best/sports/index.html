Best streaming service for sports (November 2026)

By Sam Barth · Prices checked November 9, 2026

What is the best streaming service for sports?

For the most sports in one place, YouTube TV ($82.99) or Fubo (Pro $88.99) are the strongest picks because they carry local networks plus ESPN. On a budget, pair Sling Orange ($45.99) with Peacock ($12.99) or Paramount+ ($8.99) for specific leagues.
