How does the calculator work?

Monthly savings = your current TV bill minus (the streaming plans you pick + any extra internet cost). First-year savings = monthly savings x 12 minus one-time device costs. Prices exclude taxes and promotional discounts.

How can you lower the total?

  • Use an over-the-air antenna for local channels instead of a live TV service.
  • Rotate on-demand services month to month instead of keeping all of them.
  • Check bundles: the Disney+, Hulu bundle costs 50 cents more than either service alone with ads.
