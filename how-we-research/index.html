How we research

Every price on this site comes from the provider's own pricing or help page and shows the date it was last checked. We do not invent ratings, test results or user reviews.

Where do prices come from?

From each service's official plan, sign-up or help-center page, linked next to every price table. The current data set was checked on November 9, 2026. Promotional and introductory prices are noted where the provider showed them.

Are the reviews hands-on?

No. Reviews and device guides are research-based: they summarize official specifications, plan details and published reporting. When a fact comes from a news report, the report is linked.

How are picks chosen?

Picks match a service's published lineup and price to a specific need, such as sports or kids. Affiliate relationships do not affect picks.

How do you handle news?

The Stream Desk stories are original short summaries of real events. Each one links its source, and we leave out anything we cannot verify.

Found an error?

Prices change often. If something is out of date, please contact us.
