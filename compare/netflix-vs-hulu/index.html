Netflix vs Hulu: which should you pick?

By Sam Barth · Prices checked November 9, 2026

Pick Netflix for a bigger original library and 4K; pick Hulu (ideally in the Disney+ bundle) for next-day TV episodes. With ads, Netflix is cheaper at $8.99 versus $12.49, but the $12.99 Disney+, Hulu bundle gives you two services.

What are the key differences?

  • Price with ads: Netflix $8.99, Hulu $12.49.
  • Ad-free: Netflix Standard $19.99, Hulu Premium $21.49.
  • Hulu gets many network and FX episodes the day after they air; Netflix focuses on originals and licensed seasons.
  • Only Hulu offers a live TV package (Hulu + Live TV).
