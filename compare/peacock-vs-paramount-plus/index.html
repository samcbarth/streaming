Peacock vs Paramount+: which should you pick?

By Sam Barth · Prices checked November 9, 2026

Both are low-cost sports-and-network services. Paramount+ is cheaper ad-free ($13.99 versus $19.99) and has Champions League soccer and NFL on CBS; Peacock Premium ($12.99) has NBC shows, Sunday Night Football and NBA games.

What are the key differences?

  • Cheapest plan: Peacock Select $8.99 (no sports), Paramount+ Essential $8.99.
  • Live local station: Peacock Premium Plus includes NBC; Paramount+ Premium includes CBS.
  • Pick by network: NBC and Bravo fans choose Peacock, CBS fans choose Paramount+.
