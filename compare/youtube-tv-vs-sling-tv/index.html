YouTube TV vs Sling TV: which should you pick?

By Sam Barth · Prices checked November 9, 2026

YouTube TV is the full cable replacement with local channels for $82.99. Sling TV is the budget option at $45.99 per base plan, but it has few local stations, so plan on an antenna.

What are the key differences?

  • Sling Orange & Blue together: $60.99.
  • YouTube TV includes unlimited DVR.
  • Sling has $19.99 mini plans; YouTube TV's smallest plan is $64.99.
