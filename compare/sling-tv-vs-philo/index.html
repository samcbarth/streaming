Sling TV vs Philo: which should you pick?

By Sam Barth · Prices checked November 9, 2026

Philo ($25) is cheaper but has no sports or news networks. Sling ($45.99) costs more but includes ESPN (Orange) or a broader lineup (Blue). Choose Philo only if you never watch sports.

What are the key differences?

  • Neither includes most local channels; both pair with an antenna.
  • Sling's $19.99 mini plans are an option if you need only a few channels.
