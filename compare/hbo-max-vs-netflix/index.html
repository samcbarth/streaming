HBO Max vs Netflix: which should you pick?

By Sam Barth · Prices checked November 9, 2026

HBO Max is the better value for prestige drama and Warner Bros. films, with a $10.99 ad plan and $18.49 ad-free. Netflix has a larger library overall and a cheaper ad plan at $8.99.

What are the key differences?

  • Ad-free: HBO Max Standard $18.49 versus Netflix Standard $19.99.
  • 4K: HBO Max Premium $22.99 versus Netflix Premium $26.99.
  • HBO Max offers annual billing; Netflix does not.
