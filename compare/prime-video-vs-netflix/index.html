Prime Video vs Netflix: which should you pick?

By Sam Barth · Prices checked November 9, 2026

If you already pay for Amazon Prime, Prime Video costs nothing extra (with ads) and Netflix is the add-on to judge. Ad-free, Netflix Standard is $19.99 while Prime plus Ultra is $19.98 and includes shipping benefits.

What are the key differences?

  • Prime Video standalone is $8.99, the same as Netflix with ads.
  • Prime Video has Thursday Night Football and NBA games; Netflix has occasional live events.
  • Netflix has the larger original library.
