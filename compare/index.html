Streaming comparisons

Side-by-side comparisons of popular streaming services, each with a direct answer, a price table checked November 9, 2026, and links to official pricing.

Netflix vs Hulu

Pick Netflix for a bigger original library and 4K; pick Hulu (ideally in the Disney+ bundle) for next-day TV episodes.

Netflix vs Disney+

Netflix suits mixed adult households; Disney+ suits families with kids and franchise fans.

HBO Max vs Netflix

HBO Max is the better value for prestige drama and Warner Bros.

Peacock vs Paramount+

Both are low-cost sports-and-network services.

Prime Video vs Netflix

If you already pay for Amazon Prime, Prime Video costs nothing extra (with ads) and Netflix is the add-on to judge.

Apple TV vs Netflix

Apple TV is a smaller, all-original, ad-free service at $14.99 with 4K included.

YouTube TV vs Hulu

For live TV, YouTube TV ($82.99) and Hulu + Live TV ($99.99 with Disney+ and ESPN Select) both carry local networks and ESPN.

YouTube TV vs Sling TV

YouTube TV is the full cable replacement with local channels for $82.99.

YouTube TV vs Fubo

Both are full live TV services.

Sling TV vs Philo

Philo ($25) is cheaper but has no sports or news networks.
