YouTube TV vs Hulu: which should you pick?

By Sam Barth · Prices checked November 9, 2026

For live TV, YouTube TV ($82.99) and Hulu + Live TV ($99.99 with Disney+ and ESPN Select) both carry local networks and ESPN. YouTube TV is cheaper and has unlimited DVR; Hulu + Live TV includes the Disney streaming bundle.

What are the key differences?

  • YouTube TV sells cheaper genre plans from $64.99.
  • Hulu + Live TV includes Hulu, Disney+ and ESPN Select at no extra cost.
  • NFL Sunday Ticket is an add-on only for YouTube TV.
