Apple TV vs Netflix: which should you pick?

By Sam Barth · Prices checked November 9, 2026

Apple TV is a smaller, all-original, ad-free service at $14.99 with 4K included. Netflix costs less with ads ($8.99) but $26.99 for 4K. Many people rotate Apple TV in for a month when a show they want lands.

What are the key differences?

  • Apple TV includes 4K on its only plan; Netflix reserves 4K for Premium.
  • Netflix has a far larger catalog.
  • Apple TV includes MLS matches starting in 2026.
