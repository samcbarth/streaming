Google TV Streamer guide: models, strengths and who should buy one

By Sam Barth · Updated November 9, 2026 · Research-based guide

The Google TV Streamer replaced the Chromecast line. It runs Google TV, which pulls shows from all your services into one set of recommendations, and it doubles as a Matter and Thread smart home hub. Walmart's cheaper onn devices run the same Google TV software.

What is good about Google TV Streamer?

  • Recommendations that span services
  • Built-in Chromecast casting from phones and Chrome

What should you watch out for?

  • The home screen is busy with recommendations
  • Chromecast-branded models are no longer sold new by Google

Who is it best for?

Android and Google Home users who like content suggestions across services.
