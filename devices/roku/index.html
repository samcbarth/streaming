Roku guide: models, strengths and who should buy one

By Sam Barth · Updated November 9, 2026 · Research-based guide

Roku is the simplest streaming platform: a plain grid of apps, support for almost every service, and players from a basic HD stick to the Ethernet-equipped Roku Ultra. It suits people who want a neutral device that does not push one company's services.

What is good about Roku?

  • Simple home screen that you can keep focused on apps
  • Works with nearly every streaming service
  • Free live channels in The Roku Channel

What should you watch out for?

  • The home screen shows ads and sponsored rows
  • The cheapest sticks do not support 4K

Who is it best for?

Anyone who wants an easy, brand-neutral device, including less technical family members.
