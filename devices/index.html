Streaming device guides

Which streaming device should you buy? Roku is the simplest, Fire TV fits Prime and Alexa homes, Apple TV 4K is the premium pick for iPhone users, and the Google TV Streamer suits Android and Google Home users.

Roku

Anyone who wants an easy, brand-neutral device, including less technical family members.

Amazon Fire TV

Prime members and homes that already use Alexa speakers.

Apple TV 4K

iPhone users and anyone who wants the cleanest, fastest experience.

Google TV Streamer

Android and Google Home users who like content suggestions across services.
