Apple TV 4K guide: models, strengths and who should buy one

By Sam Barth · Updated November 9, 2026 · Research-based guide

Apple TV 4K is the premium streaming box: the fastest hardware of the major platforms, no ads on the home screen, and deep iPhone, AirPlay and HomeKit integration. It costs more than sticks, so it makes most sense in Apple households.

What is good about Apple TV 4K?

  • No ads on the home screen
  • Fast, smooth interface
  • AirPlay from iPhone, iPad and Mac, plus a home hub for HomeKit

What should you watch out for?

  • Highest price of the four platforms
  • Fewest benefits if you use Android phones

Who is it best for?

iPhone users and anyone who wants the cleanest, fastest experience.
