Peacock raises prices on all three plans

Peacock Premium went to $12.99, Premium Plus to $19.99 and Select to $8.99, NBCUniversal's fourth Peacock price increase in four years.

NBCUniversal raised prices on every Peacock plan, Variety reported on August 18, 2026. Peacock Premium, which includes ads, rose from $10.99 to $12.99 a month. Premium Plus rose $3, from $16.99 to $19.99. The Select tier, which covers NBC and Bravo shows but leaves out movies, live sports and originals, went from $7.99 to $8.99.

Peacock's help center lists Premium Plus at $19.99 a month or $199.99 a year, and its price-increase page says the Apple TV and Peacock Premium Plus bundle rose from $19.99 to $22.99.

Why it matters: Premium at $12.99 is still one of the cheaper ways to get NFL and NBA games, but ad-free Peacock now costs as much as ad-free Netflix.
