The Stream Desk: streaming news

Short, sourced write-ups on streaming price changes, new services and sports rights, with what each change means for your monthly bill. Every story links to its source.

  • Walmart raises prices on its onn Google TV streamers by $10
    November 9, 2026 · Source: 9to5Google

    Walmart's onn Google TV lineup now costs $10 more per device, with the 4K Pro at $69.98 and the Full HD stick at $39.98.

  • Five NBA teams moved local games to DAZN this season
    October 28, 2026 · Source: Awful Announcing

    Five NBA teams signed exclusive local streaming deals with DAZN before the 2026-27 season, keeping only some games on over-the-air TV.

  • Netflix wins Spanish rights to the 2030 men's World Cup
    October 13, 2026 · Source: Yahoo Sports

    Netflix will stream all 104 matches of the 2030 World Cup in Spain. US rights for 2030 and 2034 have not been sold yet.

  • Disney+ and Hulu ad-free plans rise to $21.49 a month
    September 23, 2026 · Source: CBS News

    Disney raised its standalone ad-free Disney+ and Hulu plans by $2.50 to $21.49 each; the ad-free Disney+, Hulu bundle went to $21.99.

  • Apple TV streaming price rises to $14.99 a month
    August 28, 2026 · Source: CNBC

    Apple raised its Apple TV subscription from $12.99 to $14.99 a month and the annual plan from $99 to $119, effective immediately.

  • Peacock raises prices on all three plans
    August 18, 2026 · Source: Variety

    Peacock Premium went to $12.99, Premium Plus to $19.99 and Select to $8.99, NBCUniversal's fourth Peacock price increase in four years.

  • Prime Video's ad-free option becomes Ultra at $4.99 a month
    March 13, 2026 · Source: CNBC

    Amazon renamed its Prime Video ad-free add-on to Prime Video Ultra and raised it from $2.99 to $4.99 a month in the US.
