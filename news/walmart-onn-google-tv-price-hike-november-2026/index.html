Walmart raises prices on its onn Google TV streamers by $10

Walmart's onn Google TV lineup now costs $10 more per device, with the 4K Pro at $69.98 and the Full HD stick at $39.98.

Walmart has raised the price of every device in its onn Google TV streaming lineup by $10, according to a 9to5Google report published November 9, 2026. The onn 4K Pro moved from $59.98 to $69.98, the 4K Plus from $49.98 to $59.98, the 4K Streaming Stick from $39.98 to $49.98, and the Full HD Streaming Stick from $29.98 to $39.98.

The report links the increase to rising memory chip costs across consumer electronics. It is not the first increase for the line this year.

Why it matters: onn devices were the cheapest way to get Google TV software. The gap between them and name-brand sticks is now smaller, so it is worth comparing against Roku and Fire TV sale prices before buying.
