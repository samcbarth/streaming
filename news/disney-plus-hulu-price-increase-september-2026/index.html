Disney+ and Hulu ad-free plans rise to $21.49 a month

Disney raised its standalone ad-free Disney+ and Hulu plans by $2.50 to $21.49 each; the ad-free Disney+, Hulu bundle went to $21.99.

Disney raised prices across Disney+ and Hulu, CBS News reported on September 23, 2026. The standalone ad-free Disney+ Premium and Hulu Premium plans each rose by $2.50, or 13 percent, to $21.49 a month. The ad-free Disney+, Hulu Bundle Premium rose by $2, from $19.99 to $21.99.

Disney's own help pages now list Disney+ with ads at $12.49 and the Disney+, Hulu bundle with ads at $12.99.

Why it matters: ad-free Disney+ alone now costs only 50 cents less than the ad-free bundle that adds Hulu. If you pay for either service ad-free, the bundle is the better deal.
